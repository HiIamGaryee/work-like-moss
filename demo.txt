WORK LIKE MOSS - 3 MINUTE DEMO SCRIPT
Audience: CEO / HR / Manager
Device: MacBook M1, browser zoom 90-100%, DevTools closed, one tab only
Spoken pace: about 130 words per minute. Total spoken: about 390 words.

==================================================
BEFORE YOU START (do this 10 minutes early)
==================================================
[ ] npm run dev is running, site open at /login
[ ] Reset demo: log in as HR > top bar Demo menu > Reset demo (clears old approvals)
[ ] Click through once: Overview > Signals > one signal > Actions > Follow-Up > Impact (confirm each loads with data)
[ ] Stay on /login when the meeting starts
[ ] Silence notifications, close Slack/Mail, plug in charger
[ ] Backup: if the app dies, open /about (Demo Guide) and read the steps from it

NOTE: Login and logout each take about 2.4 seconds on purpose (fake auth).
Fill that gap by talking. Never stare at the spinner.

==================================================
0:00 - 0:25  THE PROBLEM  (screen: /login)
==================================================
DO:    Point at the role cards. Do not click yet.

SAY:
"Most companies find out someone is unhappy when the resignation letter arrives.
Work Like Moss turns the data HR already has, pulse surveys, exits, onboarding,
into early warnings, and then tracks whether the fix actually worked.
There are three roles: HR, Manager, and CEO. I will start as HR."

DO:    Click HR. (spinner 2.4s)

SAY (during spinner):
"Everything you see runs on a synthetic company dataset. No real employee data."

==================================================
0:25 - 0:55  OVERVIEW  (screen: / )
==================================================
DO:    Let the page load. Slowly point at the KPI row, then scroll to
       Management Attention.

SAY:
"This is HR's home. Total employees, leaver rate, engagement, new joiner
experience, open actions. But the number that matters is this queue,
Management Attention. The system has already ranked the workforce problems
by priority, so HR does not hunt through spreadsheets."

DO:    Click the top item in Management Attention (or go to Signals in the sidebar).

==================================================
0:55 - 1:30  SIGNAL + EVIDENCE  (screen: /insights then /insights/:id)
==================================================
DO:    On the signal detail page, point at: affected group, current value,
       benchmark, difference, sample size, priority.
       Scroll to Supporting Evidence, then the possible explanation.

SAY:
"Each signal shows the group, the score, the benchmark, and how far off it is.
Below that is the evidence: exit reasons, comment themes, survey trends.
We never act on one score alone. HR sees the proof first.
And privacy is built in: groups under five responses are suppressed, so we
analyse teams, never individuals."

DO (optional, 5 seconds, only if time): open the AI Draft panel, show the lens selector.
SAY: "The AI only drafts. Different HR lenses, same data. HR decides."

==================================================
1:30 - 2:10  ACTIONS  (screen: /actions)
==================================================
DO:    Sidebar > Actions. Click "Review action" on a suggested action.
       Drawer opens. Point at: owner, deadline, baseline, target, success measure.
       Close the drawer WITHOUT approving (keeps demo clean).
       Then point at an already approved action in the tracking table:
       status, delivery status, intended vs reached audience, coverage.

SAY:
"From the signal, the system suggests an action. Owner, deadline, baseline,
target, and how we will measure success. This is only a draft. HR stays the
decision maker. Once approved, we track delivery: who was meant to be reached,
who actually was, and the coverage. So the CEO can see the action really
happened, not just that someone planned it."

==================================================
2:10 - 2:40  FOLLOW-UP + IMPACT  (screen: /automations then /impact)
==================================================
DO:    Sidebar > Follow-Up. Point at due and overdue items.
       Sidebar > Impact. Open one action impact record.
       Point at baseline vs target vs follow-up result.

SAY:
"Follow-ups land in one queue, due items, overdue actions, outcome reviews,
so nothing relies on someone's memory. And here is the part most tools skip:
Impact. We separate 'did the action happen' from 'did it improve the result'.
Delivery alone proves nothing. We wait for comparable follow-up data, then
show before and after."

==================================================
2:40 - 3:00  CEO CLOSE  (screen: /ask)
==================================================
DO:    Bottom of sidebar > Sign out (spinner 2.4s) > choose CEO.
       Sidebar > Ask Work Like Moss. Click one suggested question
       (or type: "Which division needs attention first?").

SAY (during spinner):
"Managers get the same flow, locked to their own division."

SAY (once the answer shows):
"And for the CEO: a clean dashboard, plus plain-English questions answered from
the same evidence. Signal, evidence, action, follow-up, impact.
That is Work Like Moss. Happy to take questions."

==================================================
IF YOU RUN OUT OF TIME - CUT IN THIS ORDER
==================================================
1. Cut the AI Draft panel mention
2. Cut Follow-Up (go Actions > Impact directly)
3. Cut the CEO switch, say "CEO gets a dashboard and Ask" and stop

==================================================
DO NOT SAY
==================================================
- "AI decides what HR should do"  ->  say "AI drafts, HR decides"
- "Delivery proves success"       ->  say "wait for follow-up data"
- Any individual employee score or judgement
- "It is real data"               ->  it is a synthetic dataset
